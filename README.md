# Neon Overdrive

A neon arcade racing game that runs in your browser. Pick a car, choose how tough you want it, then race down a glowing synthwave highway. Collect stars to fill your boost, squeeze past traffic for combos, and beat your best score.

**Play it now:** https://alphazolo12200-tech.github.io/neon-overdrive-racing-game/

## How to play

- Tap and hold the big arrows to steer between lanes.
- Drive through stars to fill the BOOST button, then tap it for an overdrive burst.
- Pass close to traffic without touching it to build a combo and score more.
- A crash costs one life and resets your combo. When your lives run out, the race ends.

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Steer left | Left arrow button | Left arrow or A |
| Steer right | Right arrow button | Right arrow or D |
| Boost | BOOST button | Space |
| Pause | Pause screen button | P or Esc |
| Sound on/off | Sound button | M |

## Features

- **4 cars** with different speed and steering: Comet, Blaze, Mint and Star.
- **3 difficulties:** Little Driver (ages 6 and up, super easy), Zoom (easy) and Turbo (for pros).
- **3 zones** with their own colors: Sunset Strip, Midnight Coast and Aurora Highway.
- A road that curves and rushes toward you, with a glowing sun, stars in the sky and neon lines.
- Sound effects and an engine hum made with the Web Audio API, with no audio files.
- Screen shake and phone vibration on crashes.
- Your best score, car and difficulty are saved on your device.
- Private by design: no ads, no tracking, no accounts.

## Run it yourself

It is a single HTML file. Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. No installation is needed.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file. It draws the road on an HTML canvas, uses the Web Audio API for sound and localStorage to save progress. No frameworks or libraries.
